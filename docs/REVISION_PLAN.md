## Task and claim boundary

The main task is a hypothetical one-year transition at a station with local high-frequency concentration history: continuous discharge remains available, concentration is purchased sparsely and returned after a stated delay, and provisional hourly flux estimates are issued without revision. Event-window cumulative transport scores those issued estimates. This task is not claimed to reproduce a universal agency reporting clock. Historical baseline data are a prerequisite. Each evaluation year has its own local preceding history; the study does not establish multi-year sparse operation or cold-start deployment.

Original frozen experiments remain unchanged and retain their historical status. Every new experiment, revised comparison and diagnostic is a post-result revision analysis using already inspected records. Development records define the matched-factor diagnostic; the simple output rule is fixed to the already disclosed 168 h exponential time constant and arrival origin, with no test-driven retuning. The aim is to identify performance limitations and conditional value, not to search for a favorable winner.
